Privacy Policy

RLF Sunday Service · Last updated November 2, 2026

RLF Sunday Service is an internal tool used by the Risen Life Fellowship media team to schedule the church's YouTube live streams and update their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists. It is used only by church staff and volunteers on church computers.

What the app accesses

The app accesses the Risen Life Fellowship YouTube channel only when a church team member signs in with Google, and only to manage that channel's live broadcasts and videos. It does not read anyone's email, contacts, files or other Google data.

What the app stores

The sign-in token is stored only on the church computer where the sign-in happened. It is never sent to anyone else, never shared between computers and never stored on a server.

Sharing

The app does not collect, sell or share personal information, and it has no advertising or analytics.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the app's Settings ("Sign out"), or from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
