RLF Sunday Service

An internal tool for the Risen Life Fellowship media team.

RLF Sunday Service helps the church's volunteers get each Sunday ready: it turns the week's sermon notes into ProPresenter slides, prepares the worship songs, makes the sermon's title graphic, schedules the YouTube live stream and the Facebook post, and builds the podcast episode.

It runs only on the church's own computers and is used only by Risen Life Fellowship staff and volunteers. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube

When a team member signs in, the app manages the Risen Life Fellowship YouTube channel's live broadcasts: their title, description, start time, thumbnail and playlist. It doesn't upload, delete or change anything else on the channel.
