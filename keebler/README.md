# Keebler Meadows Neighbors

A zoomable map of the Keebler Meadows lots (1 to 167) for keeping track of the neighbors you meet.
Tap a lot to add the household, the people in it, how you met, tags and notes. Labeled lots show a
pin and the family name on the map.

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the site in Safari.
2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

It then opens full-screen and works offline.

## Your data

Households are saved on the phone only (browser storage for the home screen app). Nothing is sent
anywhere. Use **Neighbors, Backup** to copy a backup of everything and paste it somewhere safe;
paste it back to restore.

## Files

- `index.html` is the whole app.
- `sw.js` caches the app so it works offline. Change `CACHE` in it when you update any file.
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/`, `apple-touch-icon.png` are the install bits.
- `map.jpg` is the D.R. Horton master site plan, straightened from a photo of the printed flyer
  (revised 8/14/26). The plan is approximate and subject to change.
